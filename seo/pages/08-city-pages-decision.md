# `branding agency houston` / `branding agency austin`: skip for now

**Volume:** 170 + 140 per month. **Difficulty:** 10 to 15.

## Why skip

- Both queries trigger a Google Maps pack. The first screen is three local agencies with addresses and reviews. A Bournemouth studio with no Texas address isn't eligible for it.
- The organic results under the map pack are agencies physically in those cities, plus Clutch and DesignRush lists. A page saying "we work remotely with Austin startups" is competing against twenty firms that can take a meeting there.
- Thin city pages from a non-local business are the textbook pattern Google's helpful-content system targets. Two of them won't hurt the site. They also won't rank.

## When to revisit

- You land an Austin or Houston client and have a case study. Then one page, built around that client, is credible.
- Or you do a Texas conference run (the merch and event package angle) and have photos and a reason to be there.

Until then, the time goes further on the SaaS page.
